# Shipping Policy
Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days for metro cities and 5-8 business days for other locations. Express delivery takes 1-2 business days where available.
Q: What are the shipping charges?
A: Shipping is free on orders above Rs. 499. For smaller orders a flat fee of Rs. 49 applies. Express delivery costs Rs. 99 extra.
Q: How can I track my order?
A: Open My Orders in your account and select Track Order, or use the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email or SMS. Tracking updates within 24 hours of dispatch.
Q: Do you ship outside India?
A: No. We currently deliver only within India to all serviceable PIN codes. International shipping is not available yet.
