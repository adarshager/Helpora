# Payment Information
Q: Which payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, popular wallets and Cash on Delivery.
Q: Is Cash on Delivery available?
A: Yes, Cash on Delivery is available on orders up to Rs. 5,000 for serviceable PIN codes. A fee of Rs. 30 applies.
Q: My payment failed but money was deducted. What should I do?
A: Do not pay again. The amount is automatically reversed to your account within 5-7 business days. If it is not reversed, contact support with your transaction ID.
Q: Is it safe to pay online?
A: Yes. Payments are processed through secure, encrypted payment gateways and we never store your full card details.
