# General FAQs
Q: How can I contact customer support?
A: You can chat with this assistant, request a human agent, or email support@shopease.example. Our team is available 9 AM to 8 PM IST, Monday to Saturday.
Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Tap Login, choose Forgot Password and follow the link sent to your registered email or phone number.
Q: Do you offer warranty on products?
A: Electronics carry the manufacturer's warranty. Keep your invoice, which is available in My Orders, to claim warranty service.
Q: How do I apply a coupon code?
A: Enter the code in the Apply Coupon box at checkout. Only one coupon can be used per order and it cannot be combined with other offers.
