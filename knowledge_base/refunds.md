# Refund Policy
Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. UPI and wallet refunds are usually faster than card or net banking refunds.
Q: Where will my refund be credited?
A: Refunds go back to your original payment method. Cash on Delivery orders are refunded to your bank account or store wallet, as you choose.
Q: Do you refund shipping charges?
A: Shipping charges are refunded only if the item was damaged, defective or wrong. They are not refunded for change-of-mind returns.
Q: What if I received a damaged or wrong item?
A: Report it within 48 hours of delivery from My Orders with photos. We will arrange a free pickup and send a replacement or full refund.
