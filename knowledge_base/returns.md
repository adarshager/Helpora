# Return Policy
Q: What is your return policy?
A: You can return most items within 7 days of delivery if they are unused, unworn and in original packaging with all tags.
Q: Which items cannot be returned?
A: Innerwear, personalised items, gift cards, perishable goods and products marked Non-returnable cannot be returned.
Q: How do I start a return?
A: Go to My Orders, choose the item and tap Return, then select a reason. A pickup is scheduled within 2 business days at no cost for eligible items.
Q: Can I exchange an item instead?
A: Yes. Choose Exchange in My Orders within 7 days of delivery, subject to stock availability for the size or colour you want.
